Comic Creator – Support

Help for the Comic Creator app on iPhone and iPad.

Need help? Email comiccreator.support@icloud.com and we will reply as soon as we can. Please say which device you are using and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Getting started

How do I make my first comic?

Open the Ideas page, choose a genre, a hero, a sidekick and a villain, and tap Generate idea. You get a title, a plot and a step-by-step plan for every page. Tap Build this comic to turn it into pages you can edit. You can also tap Blank comic in the Library to start from an empty page.

How do I change a page?

Tap anything on the page to select it. Drag to move it, drag the yellow dot to resize it and the green dot to turn it. The tabs under the page add layouts, backdrops, characters, speech bubbles, sound effects and props. The Guide tab shows the plan for the page you are on.

How do I draw my own pictures?

Open the Draw tab, pick a pen and a colour, and draw on the page. Tap Done and your drawing becomes a sticker you can move, resize and rotate.

How do I put a character in a cage or behind bars?

Add the character first. Then open Props, choose Adventure and tap the cage or the jail bars. Drag the yellow dot to stretch the bars over the character.

How do I make my own character?

Open Characters and tap Build a character to choose a person or an animal, hair, clothes, colours and a mood. Tap Create from image to turn one of your own pictures into a character.

How do I make my comic play and talk?

This is part of Comic Creator Pro. Tap Live at the top of the editor. The comic plays panel by panel and the characters read their lines aloud, each in their own voice. To change whether a character has a female or male voice, open Characters and use the voice choice on the character.

How do I record my own voice?

Tap a speech bubble, scroll down to Your voice and tap Record this line. Read the words out loud and tap Stop. The live view then plays your recording for that bubble. The first time, your device asks to use the microphone. If recording does not start, a grown-up can check Settings → Comic Creator → Microphone.

Saving and sharing

Where are my comics saved?

On your device, inside the app. Nothing is uploaded. Deleting the app deletes your comics, so save a PDF of anything you want to keep.

How do I save or print a comic?

Tap Export, then Save, share or print PDF. A grown-up check appears first, then you can print, save to Files or send the PDF.

Why does the app ask a grown-up to type a number?

Buying the Pro upgrade and sharing a comic outside the app are protected by a grown-ups-only check, so children cannot do either by accident.

Comic Creator Pro

What does Pro add?

Room for 20 comics instead of 10, extra characters and builder parts, storyboard editing, illustrated scene backdrops, the full props library, extra lettering and sound-effect styles, and the live view with voices and your own voice recordings. It is a one-time purchase with no subscription.

I bought Pro but it is not unlocked

Tap Go Pro, then Restore purchases at the bottom of the screen, and pass the grown-up check. Make sure the device is signed in to the same Apple Account that made the purchase.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com to request one.

Problems

The app says my library is full

Comic Creator holds 10 comics and Pro holds 20. Save a PDF of an old comic, then delete it from the Library to make room.

Something looks wrong or the app will not open

Close the app completely and open it again. If that does not help, restart your device. If the problem continues, email us at the address above.
