Comic Creator – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Comic Creator ("the app") is a comic-book maker for children. We take children's privacy seriously. The short version: the app does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information.

What the app stores on your device

To keep your work, the app saves these things on the device only: the comics you make (pages, words, characters and backdrops), the characters you build and the names you give them, any pictures you choose to add, any voice recordings you make, and your last idea-generator choices. This information never leaves the device unless a grown-up chooses to share a comic (see below). We cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Photos and camera

You can add your own picture as a character or onto a page. The app only sees a picture you pick yourself, or a photo you take at that moment. The camera permission is asked for only if you choose to take a photo. Pictures are kept inside the app on your device and are never uploaded or sent to us.

Microphone and voice recordings

In Comic Creator Pro you can record your own voice reading a speech bubble, to play back in the live view of your comic. The microphone permission is asked for only when you first tap Record. Recordings are saved with the comic on your device and are never uploaded or sent to us. Deleting the recording, the comic or the app deletes them. A recording only leaves the device if a grown-up chooses to share the comic as a web page.

What we do not collect

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No names, email addresses, locations, contacts or device identifiers
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No third-party software development kits

Internet use

The app works fully offline and makes no network requests of its own. The only network activity is the App Store, when a grown-up chooses to buy or restore the Pro upgrade.

Sharing a comic

A comic can be saved as a PDF or web page, printed, or shared using the standard iOS share sheet. This is protected by a grown-ups-only check. The file goes only where the grown-up chooses to send it, and we never receive a copy.

In-app purchases

The Comic Creator Pro upgrade is a one-time purchase, is handled entirely by Apple and is protected by a grown-ups-only check inside the app. We never see or store payment details. Apple's own privacy policy applies to the purchase: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Children

The app is designed for children and is safe for them to use. Because we collect no personal information, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 or under 16, and there is no way for a child to send anything to us or to anyone else through the app without a grown-up.

Changes to this policy
